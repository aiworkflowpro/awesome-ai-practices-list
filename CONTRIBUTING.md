# Contributing

Recommend an account or report a correction through [issues](https://github.com/aiworkflowpro/awesome-ai-practices-list/issues).

## Recommend a source

Include the account or post URL, the most relevant topic, and a short explanation of what readers can learn. Disclose any affiliation. We look for original demonstrations, first-hand observations and specific, attributable evidence.

## Report a correction

Include the source ID or edition URL, the affected sentence, and evidence supporting the correction. Broken links, missing attribution and removal requests are welcome. Authors may request review of material that concerns their work.

## Suggest an edit

Open a pull request with a focused change and a clear reason. Keep attribution and qualifications intact; distinguish an author's claim from an independently tested result. Accepted corrections also reach the website so future editions preserve them.

Do not submit original post text, third-party media, confidential information or unsupported claims. Please be constructive and respectful; responses are on a best-effort basis.
