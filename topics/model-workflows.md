## Latest weekly perspective · 2026-w38

### The week in model workflows

This week's editor-selected posts centered on new model and tooling releases—Qwen's open-weight Qwen-Image-2.1 image model, Google's Gemini 3.8 Live audio models and AlphaGenome Atlas, and OpenAI's Astra for Law—alongside Anthropic's disclosures on AI R&amp;D measurement, model optimization, and evaluation partnerships. #### What changed

- Qwen announced Qwen-Image-2.1 as an open-weight 7B image model unifying generation and editing, claiming native RGBA transparency, support for up to 10 reference images, and improved text and portrait aesthetics over its predecessor. [Qwen · 2101659302792679789](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2101659302792679789) [Qwen · 2101659324980625533](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2101659324980625533)
- Qwen said Qwen-Image-2.1 is supported in ComfyUI, with day-0 support from vLLM for serving generation, editing, and transparent output. [Qwen · 2101670814953455780](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2101670814953455780) [Qwen · 2101670804794884242](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2101670804794884242)
- Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking, describing upgraded reasoning, near real-time visual understanding, automatic detection across 97 languages, and background tool calling, with Extended Thinking reasoning and speaking in parallel. [Google DeepMind · 2099907447313899684](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2099907447313899684) [Google AI · 2099908000924193124](https://x.com/GoogleAI/status/2099908000924193124)
- Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas, an interactive genomics platform mapping over 2,500 regulatory patterns, which it says is freely accessible and already used by researchers at the Broad Institute and University of Exeter. [Google DeepMind · 2100586768248562157](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2100586768248562157) [Google DeepMind · 2100586760036143330](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2100586760036143330) [Google DeepMind · 2100586770572177551](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2100586770572177551) [Google AI · 2101008284010602924](https://x.com/GoogleAI/status/2101008284010602924)
- OpenAI launched Astra for Law, a GPT-6 Astra-powered offering for lawyers and legal technology firms, initially available to selected firms via Trusted Access in ChatGPT and Codex with API access coming soon, plus 26 partner and 47 community legal plugins. [OpenAI · 2100679992720142459](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2100679992720142459) [OpenAI · 2100680000072773702](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2100680000072773702) [OpenAI · 2100679997862330735](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2100679997862330735)
- Anthropic published three internal measurements of AI development—AI-performed R&amp;D, agent oversight, and compute allocation—and said it is partnering with Accenture on independent frontier AI evaluation with each company expecting to invest at least $1 billion over five years. #### Worth trying

- Practitioners could trial Qwen-Image-2.1's editing workflows, such as generating a storyboard from a three-view character reference, using the available ComfyUI integration. [Qwen · 2101670814953455780](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2101670814953455780) [Qwen · 2101659321549660610](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2101659321549660610)
- Developers with audio-interaction needs could test Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking in public preview via the Gemini API on Google AI Studio. [Google AI · 2099908003071729974](https://x.com/GoogleAI/status/2099908003071729974)
- Life science teams could apply to Anthropic's beta Life Sciences Verification Program, which provides access to models including Mythos under new biological-work safeguards. [Anthropic · 2100646837799834096](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2100646837799834096)
- Researchers interested in protein design could look into Anthropic and Adaptyv Bio's competition, which plans experimental validation of over 5,000 designs with Claude credits and partner funding. [Anthropic · 2100701582744797347](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2100701582744797347)

#### Watch next

- Whether Anthropic's published R&amp;D measurement methodology and its independent evaluation partnership with Accenture yield third-party verifiable results remains to be seen.
