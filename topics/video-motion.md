## Latest weekly perspective · 2026-w38

### The week in video motion

This week's editor-selected posts are dominated by incremental tooling updates rather than a single breakthrough, spanning Blender's extensions and LTS maintenance, Blackmagic's camera software, Adobe's generative media inside Premiere, and Framer's prompt-driven 3D and motion components. #### What changed

- Blender reported that its Extensions Platform will soon be expanded to support asset libraries, with the accompanying details stating that only CC0 assets will be accepted, that hosting will sit on Blender's servers, and that sharing requires a submission request form. [Blender 🔶 · 2100960387654992092](https://x.com/Blender/status/2100960387654992092) [Blender 🔶 · 2100960390112809452](https://x.com/Blender/status/2100960390112809452) [Blender 🔶 · 2100960391081627986](https://x.com/Blender/status/2100960391081627986)
- Blender announced LTS maintenance releases, listing Blender 5.2.2 LTS with 37 fixes and Blender 4.5.14 LTS with a single fix, with the changelog available at the linked download page. [Blender 🔶 · 2100177114175623217](https://x.com/Blender/status/2100177114175623217)
- Blackmagic Design announced Blackmagic Camera for iOS 3.5, which it says adds support for iPhone 18 Pro models including variable aperture control on the 48MP fusion main camera and Apple ProRes recording using pro video storage. [Blackmagic Design · 2100772425923453082](https://x.com/Blackmagic_News/status/2100772425923453082)
- Blackmagic Design also reported Blackmagic Camera 10.3, which it says adds SMPTE-2110 video support to the URSA Cine 12K LF along with generation 6 color science, the URSA Cine Mount B4 and the URSA Cine Studio Viewfinder, and 10.2.3, which it says improves startup performance and adds a quiet record function on the URSA Cine 17K 65. [Blackmagic Design · 2099446485037498784](https://x.com/Blackmagic_News/status/2099446485037498784) [Blackmagic Design · 2099379443882192897](https://x.com/Blackmagic_News/status/2099379443882192897)
- Adobe Video said Premiere now includes a Generative Media Tool that can generate b-roll and other video assets using reference frames from the timeline across several selectable models, and separately that Generate Sound can produce sound effects from a description with voice-based refinement of timing and intensity. [Adobe Video · 2099543703454924876](https://x.com/AdobeVideo/status/2099543703454924876) [Adobe Video · 2099906088363573602](https://x.com/AdobeVideo/status/2099906088363573602)
- Framer described prompting to build, restyle and animate with 3D transforms, including a 360-degree rotation, and published detailed component prompts such as a draggable 3D gallery card stack and an animated five-stat carousel with count-up digits. [Framer · 2101002674804019282](https://x.com/framer/status/2101002674804019282) [Framer · 2100666871754440975](https://x.com/framer/status/2100666871754440975) [Framer · 2100666907074703767](https://x.com/framer/status/2100666907074703767) [Framer · 2100666919045259684](https://x.com/framer/status/2100666919045259684)

#### Worth trying

- For teams working in Blender, the CC0-only asset library plan may be worth watching as a future source of shareable assets, though the posts describe an upcoming expansion rather than something usable today. [Blender 🔶 · 2100960387654992092](https://x.com/Blender/status/2100960387654992092) [Blender 🔶 · 2100960390112809452](https://x.com/Blender/status/2100960390112809452)
- Motion designers evaluating prompt-driven layout and animation could inspect the Framer Agent examples, including the 3D gallery card and animated metric prompts, as reference specifications for interactive web motion without assuming output quality. [Framer · 2100666907074703767](https://x.com/framer/status/2100666907074703767) [Framer · 2100666919045259684](https://x.com/framer/status/2100666919045259684)
