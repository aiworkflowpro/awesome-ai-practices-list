### The week in video motion

This week's editor-selected posts center on agent-facing authoring features in Framer and Remotion, alongside GSAP showcase picks and a set of vendor announcements in drawing, mobile editing, and 3D texture workflows. [Framer · 2102408056856609042](https://x.com/framer/status/2102408056856609042) [Jonny Burger · 2103386024026288495](https://x.com/JNYBGR/status/2103386024026288495) [GSAP · 2103449488094589288](https://x.com/greensock/status/2103449488094589288) [Rive · 2102455532959199499](https://x.com/rive_app/status/2102455532959199499) [Adobe Video · 2102481097669910892](https://x.com/AdobeVideo/status/2102481097669910892) [School of Motion · 2102428103742009685](https://x.com/SchoolOfMotion/status/2102428103742009685)

#### What changed

- Framer introduced Skills, which let users save reusable instructions for their design system, writing style, or CMS workflow and reference pages, components, and styles so the agent follows their designs. [Framer · 2102408056856609042](https://x.com/framer/status/2102408056856609042)

#### Worth trying

- Teams already using Framer Skills could try the /launch-check skill in the Agent chat, which Framer describes as checking accessibility, SEO, links, content, and responsive layouts before launch. [Framer · 2103923557956993093](https://x.com/framer/status/2103923557956993093)
- Framer users can create their own skills with /skills or copy and paste existing ones for effects, SEO, brand voice, and similar workflows, per Framer's walkthrough. [Framer · 2103515479675388092](https://x.com/framer/status/2103515479675388092)
- Framer users wanting the agent to default to their own components could point a skill at a page via a page mention, which Framer says tells the agent to use that page when designing something new. [Framer · 2102765990039998524](https://x.com/framer/status/2102765990039998524)
- Framer users can learn how positioning works by reviewing what each layer is measured against across relative, absolute, fixed, and sticky modes, per Framer's explainer. [Framer · 2103189846332432751](https://x.com/framer/status/2103189846332432751)
- Those new to responsive layout in Framer could follow the vendor's walkthrough on making layouts flex, wrap, and stack, preserving hierarchy across screen sizes, and testing responsive behavior. [Framer · 2102871367163199900](https://x.com/framer/status/2102871367163199900)

#### Watch next

- In its described release, Remotion says it made SVG paths editable via a /remotion-interactivity skill, a capability claim from the vendor rather than an independent evaluation. [Jonny Burger · 2103386024026288495](https://x.com/JNYBGR/status/2103386024026288495)
- Rive announced that Freehand Brush Tools, including Brush, Pencil, Eraser, Bucket, and Shape Bend, are live for everyone to draw by hand and then animate. [Rive · 2102455532959199499](https://x.com/rive_app/status/2102455532959199499)
- Adobe Video stated that Premiere mobile is now officially available on Android via the Google Play Store, framing it as studio-level editing on mobile. [Adobe Video · 2102481097669910892](https://x.com/AdobeVideo/status/2102481097669910892)
- School of Motion highlighted Pixel Art Studio, which it describes as bringing a low-res texture painting workflow across a 3D model into Blender, as covered on Motion Mondays. [School of Motion · 2102428103742009685](https://x.com/SchoolOfMotion/status/2102428103742009685)
